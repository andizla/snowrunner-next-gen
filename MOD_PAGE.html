PC only, manual install. Subscribing in game does nothing: download the zip from this page.

 

Graphics modules for SnowRunner, installed from one window: sharper shadows with straight edges, ground truth ambient occlusion and bounce light, reflections on paint, glass, water and wet ground, skies made from photographs of real skies, headlight shadows, debris and splashes that stay on the ground, volumetric fog with sun shafts, more of the game's weather, the night sky with real stars, and a photo colour grade. Tick the modules you want and click Apply. Restore puts the game's own files back. 

 

Called SnowRunner Next Gen before October 2026. It needs no loader or injector: the shaders go into the game's own shader cache, and SnowRunner Shadows is one small DLL next to the game. It is NOT DLSS5.

 

The full guide, a PDF with a part for beginners and every card and setting in depth: SnowRunner_Next_Gen_Guide.pdf

 

NEW IN 1.1.0

New
Skies, on by default: 49 of the game's sky domes, cloud bands and night layers, every region's, made anew from photographs of real skies, each at the size and brightness of the texture it replaces. The photographs' sun is taken out, so the game's sun is the only one, and the clouds still drift. The aurora is green, as a real one is. The sky textures come as a download of their own, the sky pack.
Reflection cubes, on by default: the painted sky cubes that paint, glass and chrome mirror, made anew from 21 CC0 panoramas for every region's days, dusks, dawns and nights.
Debris and splashes, on by default: leaves, twigs, dirt and stones thrown up by the wheels lie on the ground for 30 to 40 seconds, mud, water and snow splashes throw four times the drops, the water off a spinning wheel flies farther, and snow spray hangs in the air.
Headlight shadows, off by default: posts, trees and rocks in the beam cast shadows away from the truck's lamps. Tested in the game and still being tuned.
Map days, off by default: Maine, British Columbia, Austria and Washington show days that fit them (Maine Michigan's instead of Yukon's and Alaska's snow), map by map where a map differs.
Weather: Drifting clouds (on), so the cloud pictures that stood still turn slowly; Far land (off), so 46 states that stopped drawing the ground at 800 metres draw it as far as their fog reaches.
Next Gen logo: Start-up (off) starts the game without the warning and the publisher logos, about 18 seconds sooner.
More kinds of smoke (off) and Sun glitter in shade (off, experimental).


Changed
Photo grade also takes out the game's lens dirt over bright sky.
Grass reach grows the grass in instead of letting it pop up at the game's 64 metre edge.
Brighter stars leaves dusk and dawn at the game's level.
Night sky: the Milky Way's faint edge fades in smoothly instead of in hard-edged islands.


SnowRunner Shadows .dll
With Next Gen Gameplay or Next Gen Tire Inflation installed, their NEXT GEN menu (F2) gets a Graphics tab next to theirs with every switch of the F keys; while that menu is there, F4 to F11 are silent, as other mods use some of the same keys (KeysWithMenu=1 in SnowRunnerShadows.ini keeps them). Next Gen Graphics alone has no menu, and its F keys work as before.
Faster starts: the originals for the F8 and F11 comparisons are made the first time a key asks for them.
After a game update it finds the game's texture value itself, so upscaled textures keep loading.
It no longer writes into DXGI's shared method tables, which together with another program's hooks could crash the game.
No old picture in the shadows after a video setting changes.

WHAT IS IN IT

34 modules, 27 of them on by default. Mix and match as you want, some effects require other, the installer handles this for you.

 

Light and shadows: SnowRunner Shadows with a larger shadow texture if you want one, straight shadow edges with blended cascade seams, a blocker search for soft shadow ends, contact shadows, GTAO with a wide search, sky ambient, less fill light, bounce light from sunlit snow, mud and paint, and headlight shadows. 1x shadow resolution is fine, more if you have the GPU.

 

Reflections and water: paint, glass and chrome mirror the scene, headlights show in them at night, the sky cubes they mirror are made from photographs, water and wet ground mirror banks, trees and trucks, deeper water keeps its own colour, and river waves light up against a low sun.

 

Atmosphere and image: skies made from photographs of real skies, volumetric fog with a glow toward the sun and sun shafts, moving cloud shadows, drifting clouds and more weather sets on every map, days that fit the regions that borrowed another region's, smoke that glows against the sun with a lit and a shaded side, sharper particles, debris and splashes that stay on the ground, the night sky from NASA's star map with brighter stars, a tonemap with more contrast, a bloom without haze, the photo grade for more realistic colours, and a start without the intro.

 

Scenery LOD increase, off by default: scenery detail that pops in less, and grass drawn farther out. I have these maxed on my own game, they help with the shadow/ao pop in.

 

The (?) on each card says what the module does, what to look for, and what it costs.

 

WHAT YOU NEED

- SnowRunner for PC. Made and tested on the Steam version. The Epic Games version's folders are handled since 1.0.1. On a game version whose files differ, a module that does not fit is left out and the program says so.

- Nothing else. The zip brings everything the program needs. For the Skies module, also the sky pack (SnowRunnerNextGen_Skies.zip, about 100 MB).

 

INSTALL

1. Close SnowRunner.

2. Download the zip and the sky pack from this page, unzip the folder anywhere, unzip the sky pack into the same folder, and run SnowRunnerNextGen.exe.

3. The program finds the game through Steam, Epic Games or the Microsoft Store. If it does not, pick the game folder.

4. Pick the modules, then click Apply. The first Apply reads your game's own shaders and prepares the modules from them, which takes a minute or two.

 

Restore originals puts every file back as it was and takes SnowRunner Shadows out. The copies of the original files stay in %LOCALAPPDATA%\SnowRunnerNextGen (2 to 4 GB): to remove the mod for good, delete that folder by hand after Restore.

 

IN THE GAME

F8 switches every shader effect off and on, for a quick comparison. F4 does that for the contact shadows alone, F5 switches the ambient occlusion pass between half and full size, F6 the reflection pass, F9 the wet ground reflections, F10 the bounce light, and F11 switches between GTAO and the game's own ambient occlusion. The keys do nothing while Alt, Ctrl or Shift is held. With Next Gen Gameplay or Next Gen Tire Inflation installed, the same switches are on a Graphics tab next to theirs in their NEXT GEN menu (F2), and the F keys stay silent, as other mods use some of them; KeysWithMenu=1 in SnowRunnerShadows.ini keeps them. Next Gen Graphics alone has no menu, and its keys work as before. These will be removed later, only F8 will remain.

 

OTHER MODS AND GAME UPDATES

Compatible with all mods if you install Next Gen last. The program keeps a copy of each file before its first change and builds from those copies. When another mod or a game update has changed one of them since, Apply and Restore ask first: take the file as it is now as the original, or leave it out. A game update or the store's file check puts the game's own files back; run the program again and click Apply. This also means there's a space requirement.

 

GOOD TO KNOW

- No game files are included. The modules are made from your own game's files when you click Apply.

-Each of the game's files it changes is written next to the old one, read back and checked before it takes its place.

-Compatible with all my mods except GTAO. This mod improves and replaced that mod.

-Multiplayer untested but probably works if everyone has the mod.

- Source code and build steps: https://github.com/andizla/snowrunner-next-gen (GPL-3.0).

 

KNOWN

The fog sun shafts are experimental: with a low sun they also show over the truck and other things close to the camera. A fix is planned for a later version.

Headlight shadows are off by default and still being tuned. They come from what the camera sees: a caster outside the picture casts nothing.

With Map days map by map on, a game update that replaces initial.pak alone stops Maine's maps from loading until Apply runs again.

 

PLANNED
