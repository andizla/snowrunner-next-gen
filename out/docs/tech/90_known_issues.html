Limits by design

CardLimit
Object reflections, Water reflections, Wet ground reflections, Contact shadows, GTAO, Bounce lightScreen space: they know only what the camera sees. Things off screen or hidden behind something are not reflected, cast no contact shadow and no occlusion; reflections fade at the screen's edges.
Object reflections, Wet ground reflections, Bounce lightThey read the previous frame, so they lag one frame and have nothing for pixels just uncovered.
Bounce light (off by default)In places, bright snow next to dark wood for example, it comes out too strong.
Volumetric fog, Sun shafts (off by default)The fog does not know what stands in front of each step, so with a low sun the shafts also show over the truck and other things close to the camera.
Water reflections, Sun glitter in shade (off by default)Where it was tried the game's own shadow test said the water was lit, so nothing changed.
Sun glow through waves8 of the 20 river shaders do not read the sun's shadow map and get no glow.
Shadow edges, seam ditherThe band where cascades blend relies on the game's TAA; detail smaller than a shadow texel cannot be rebuilt.
Map days, map by map for MaineMaine's own sets live in initial.pak. If a game update replaces it while Maine's level files name the sets, Maine does not load until Apply has run again.
Next Gen logo, Start-upThe game still loads behind the splash; the time saved is the part of the intro that outlasted the loading (about 16 s in the test).
